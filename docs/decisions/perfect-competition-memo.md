# Perfect Competition — Decision Memo

## Recommendation

I recommend planting 10 tomato beds, 20 carrot beds, and 30 mesclun beds because this combination maximizes profit based on the model. Even though the farm has 64 beds available, the optimal plan only uses 60. Leaving four beds empty is okay because filling those additional beds would not increase profit. This showed me that using all of the available space does not necessarily mean making the most money.

## Management Priority

If I could change one constraint, I would increase the carrot cap first. Carrots are already at their maximum of 20 beds, but an additional carrot bed could add about $352 in profit compared with about $246 for an additional mesclun bed. Tomatoes would not be the priority because the model already shows that planting beyond 10 tomato beds would not be profitable based on marginal cost.

## What Could Change My Recommendation

One factor that could change my recommendation is labor cost. Labor is already a major factor in the model, especially for tomatoes. If labor costs continue to rise, the marginal cost of producing each crop could also increase. This could change which crops and how many beds are most profitable, so the 10/20/30 recommendation would need to be reevaluated.
